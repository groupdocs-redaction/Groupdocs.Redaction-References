### Metoder
| Metod | Beskrivning |
| :- | :- |
| [failed](/redaction/python-net/groupdocs.redaction/redactionresult/failed/#description) | Initierar en ny RedactionResult-instans med misslyckad status. |
| [partial](/redaction/python-net/groupdocs.redaction/redactionresult/partial/#description) | Skapar en ny [`RedactionResult`](/redaction/python-net/groupdocs.redaction/redactionresult/) instans med delvis tillämpad status. |

| [successful](/redaction/python-net/groupdocs.redaction/redactionresult/successful/) | Initierar en ny instans av RedactionResult med en tillämpad (framgångsrik) status. |
